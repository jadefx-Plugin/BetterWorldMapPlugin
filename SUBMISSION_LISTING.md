## Summary

Browse RuneScape maps, search bundled monster and NPC locations, inspect travel and dungeon links, and customize local map markers and filters.

## What it does

Better World Map adds a movable, resizable map window with an overview, searchable reference locations, categorized map icons/teleports, supported underground maps, quest details and monster-drop references. Hover cards show recorded requirements or combat levels where available. Nearby monster dots use only loaded, exposed client entities.

Press M to open the map. Drag to pan, use the wheel to zoom, and open the cog for pin colors, sizes, normal/walking transparency, shortcuts and drop-menu preferences. Settings save locally. All map/teleport controls change plugin UI; they do not walk, teleport or send gameplay actions. the Controls page explains rebinding it manually.

Bundled locations and drop tables are historical references, not guaranteed live spawns, complete unlock checks or loot predictions. Some destinations are approximate area anchors, some maps are hidden from the dropdown, and not every internal ladder has a verified link. Contact email

## Data protection — case 1: stays local

Data used during play: exposed local player position/movement, loaded NPC information, quest/skill checks and user interactions with this plugin. These support map position, nearby monster dots, local reference/filter information and controls.

Data retained: window geometry, selected map, panel visibility, filters, marker colors/transparency/size, shortcut and optional minimap click override. They are stored in this plugin's local PersistentDB record. Loaded NPC caches and map-view history are session-only. No data is sent to the developer or another endpoint; other-player information is not collected or exported.

Reference data and artwork ship with the plugin. Wiki/source links are attribution, not runtime requests. This description covers Better World Map.
